What I do

Governance that people can actually work with.

Most of my work sits where business rules, source systems and regulatory deadlines meet. These are the six things I'm usually brought in for.

Data quality controls

Rule repositories and validation frameworks that catch errors before they reach a report, or a regulator.

Root-cause investigation

Tracing exceptions back through source systems, transformations and loads until the real cause is found and fixed.

Metadata & lineage

Recording where data comes from, how it changes and who owns it, so people can trust what they are looking at.

Governance operating model

Intake processes, ownership and standards that teams adopt, including rolling out an Informatica-based governance model.

Regulatory reporting

Financial, tax and compliance data prepared for FCA submission, with checks timed around fixed deadlines.

Migration integrity

SAP ECC to S/4HANA migrations with validation and reconciliation built in from the first load.

Casework

From the issue log.

Four problems I've worked on, written up the way a governance team would log them. Client names are left out.

DQ-01 Regulatory reporting Controlled

Catching reporting errors before the regulator does

Issue
Reports for FCA submission draw on financial, tax and compliance data from many source systems, and errors had to be caught before each fixed submission date.
Action
Built a data-quality rule repository and validation framework with SMEs, developers and client stakeholders, and made root-cause investigation part of the routine.
Impact
90% accuracy on checks run ahead of submission.
DQ-02 S/4HANA migration Resolved

The tax field nobody could load

Issue
A critical migration blocker on a tax-related field. Senior engineers had already tried the usual routes and given up.
Action
Researched alternative BAPI approaches independently, then worked them through with global SAP and ABAP teams.
Impact
A working fix where the existing approaches had failed.
DQ-03 Finance data Resolved

Finance values that changed after load

Issue
After go-live, S/4HANA was silently altering finance values once they had been loaded.
Action
Traced the change to system-level behaviour and put an interim workaround in place so the business could keep moving.
Impact
Drove the permanent fix with SAP.
DQ-04 Operating model Adopted

One governance model across many workstreams

Issue
Several regulatory reporting workstreams, each with its own habits for intake, metadata and ownership.
Action
Advised stakeholders on governance practice, standardised the intake process and kept business, governance and technical teams in step.
Impact
A new Informatica-based governance model adopted across the workstreams.
