03 · Case notes

A few problems worth telling.

Client details are left out. The problems and what fixed them are not.

01

Catching reporting errors before the regulator does

Context
A regulatory reporting programme drawing financial, tax-related and compliance data from many source systems, with fixed FCA submission dates.
Approach
Built a data-quality rule repository and validation framework with SMEs, developers and client stakeholders, and made root-cause investigation part of the routine.
Outcome
90% accuracy on checks run ahead of submission.

02

The tax field nobody could load

Context
A critical S/4HANA migration blocker on a tax-related field. Senior engineers had already tried the usual routes and given up.
Approach
Researched alternative BAPI approaches on my own, then worked it through with global SAP and ABAP teams.
Outcome
A working fix where the existing approaches had failed.

03

Finance values that changed after load

Context
After go-live, S/4HANA was silently altering finance values once they had been loaded.
Approach
Traced the change to system-level behaviour and put an interim workaround in place so the business could keep moving.
Outcome
Drove the permanent fix with SAP.

04

One governance model across many workstreams

Context
Several regulatory reporting workstreams, each with its own habits for intake, metadata and ownership.
Approach
Advised client stakeholders on governance practice, standardised the intake process and kept business, governance and technical teams in step.
Outcome
Adoption of a new Informatica-based governance model across the workstreams.
